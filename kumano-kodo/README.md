# Kumano Kodo: Nakahechi and Ōhechi

Live: https://sophiachen-geo.github.io/no-one-is-an-island/kumano-kodo/

Every place from the Kumano notes (15 numbered places, 35 spots) on both trails, over GSI terrain.
A static, self-contained page (`index.html`); deploy.yml copies this folder into the site as-is.

- Terrain and coastline: GSI 10 m DEM (DEM10B), 出典：国土地理院
- Nakahechi: OpenStreetMap Kumano Kodo relations (Takijiri–Hongū, Kogumotori-goe, Ōkumotori-goe and branches)
- Ōhechi and Tanabe–Takijiri: reconstructed through the route's stations and passes by pedestrian routing (dashed, approximate); no open survey of these alignments exists
- Rivers and the Esumi–Sangodai motorway under construction: © OpenStreetMap contributors, retrieved 1 Oct 2026
