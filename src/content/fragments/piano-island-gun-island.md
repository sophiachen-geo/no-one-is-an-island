Gulangyu got 600 pianos. Western missionaries and merchants arrived in the 19th century and, among other things, brought their instruments. The island now has more pianos per capita than anywhere on earth. It has a Piano Museum, an Organ Museum, and streets narrow enough that music drifts from every open window.

Xiju got the densest network of military tunnels on the planet. The ROC Army arrived in 1949 and spent decades drilling through granite. There is a slogan carved directly into a cliff face: *Man Will Conquer Nature* (人定勝天).

Both islands were remade by outsiders who arrived by sea with a project in mind. One project was music. The other was war. Both left permanent marks in the rock.
